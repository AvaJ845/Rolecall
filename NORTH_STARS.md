# Rolecall — the two North Stars

Every build decision must serve **both**. When they conflict, the product North Star wins
on *what we ship*; the Apple North Star wins on *how it looks and feels*.

## 1. Product North Star

> A designer's trustworthy shortcut to every live product-design role at a real tech
> company — each listing verified working, straight from the company, never a ghost job.
> Free to search, always. It respects someone mid-search: no meter, no fake urgency, no
> dead links, no reselling their attention.
>
> It must never become a stale SEO aggregator or a feed that profits from keeping people
> searching.

Operationalised:

- **Completeness** — within our vertical + region, if a role is live on the company's own
  ATS, it is in Rolecall within an hour. We publish our measured coverage number.
- **Freshness** — every card shows `verified live · Xm ago`. A role that leaves the
  company feed disappears from Rolecall on the next ingest.
- **Zero dead links** — target ≥ 98% verified-live accuracy, < 1 dead-link report per 100
  opens. This is the gate; if the engine can't hit it, there is no product.
- **Source-direct** — every tap lands on the company's own application page. No
  in-between, no re-hosted apply forms, no tracking redirect.
- **No account at all** — not even optional Sign in with Apple. Nothing to log into,
  nothing to sync, nothing to leak. Saved roles and alert preferences live only on device.

## 2. Apple North Star (the only realistic path from zero to visible)

> We have no audience, no social following, and no ad budget. Apple editorial featuring is
> the single realistic path to visibility. Apple's editors consistently favour calm,
> privacy-first, no-account utilities with exceptional icon and design craft.

Operationalised:

- **Calm** — one screen, one job, no feed, no streaks, no badges, no push spam, no
  gamification. It opens, it shows fresh roles, it gets out of the way.
- **Privacy-first, provably** — no analytics SDKs, no third-party trackers, no ad
  networks. App Privacy "Data Not Collected". This is a feature we say out loud.
- **No account** — no sign-in of any kind; it is exactly what Apple's editors reward.
- **Icon + design craft** — the icon is a launch-blocking deliverable, not a fast-follow.
  Typography, spacing, motion, dark mode, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver — all first-pass, not
  retrofit. The audience (designers) and the gatekeeper (Apple's design-led editorial
  team) are the same kind of people. Build for their eye.
- **A story worth telling** — "finding a design job, minus the ghost jobs and the
  200-applicant listings." Themed hooks: New Year career features (January), graduation
  (May–June), "Tools for Creatives" / "Apps with Great Design" roundups.

## The test for any feature

1. Does it make the board more complete, fresher, or more trustworthy? (Product NS)
2. Could you demo it to an Apple editor without apologising for a single pixel? (Apple NS)

If not both, it doesn't ship in v1.
